09 // QUESTIONS

FAQ

How does a keypad-phone elder trigger Urimai?

They speed-dial '9' and speak in their mother tongue. Gemini classifies Scam (Kavach) vs Civic (Urimai) and sends an Actionable Kit to their child's web dashboard.

What if the son/daughter lives in another city?

They can use Solution 1 (Digital-First Bypass: 1-click copy-paste into CM Helpline / Mudhalvarin Mugavari / CPGRAMS) or Solution 2 (Local Proxy: forward a secure WhatsApp upload link to a neighbor or e-Seva center).

Does the PDF support Tamil, Hindi and regional scripts?

Yes! Our Unicode-safe A4 PDF generator renders Official English + any of 23 Indian scripts, embeds the uploaded street photo, and adds the 5-resident signature block.

Does it work offline / without an API key?

Yes. The core engine runs locally in the browser on GitHub Pages and upgrades to Gemini 2.5 Flash automatically when deployed with GEMINI_API_KEY.
